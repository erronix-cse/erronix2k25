Ballistic Bash

Ballistic Bash is a thrilling non-technical event that tests participants’ balance, focus, and teamwork. Teams must carefully balance a ball on a pen and move from start to finish without dropping it. It’s a game of patience, precision, and coordination — one slip can send you back to the start!

Event Rules & Guidelines

1. Teams and Players

  • A total of five (5) teams will participate in the event.
  • Each team shall consist of three (3) players.
  • All teams will be provided with an equal opportunity to participate.

2. Equipment: Balls and Pens

  • Each team will be provided with five (5) balls.
  • Each team member will receive one (1) pen to balance the ball.
  • Only the materials provided by the organizers may be used during the event.

3. Throw and Aim Instructions

  • All team members must stand behind the start line before beginning.
  • Participants must balance the ball on the pen and move carefully from the start line to the end line.
  • If the ball falls to the ground at any point, the team must restart from the start line.
  • Crossing the start line before beginning or during the attempt will result in a restart or disqualification as determined by the judges.

4. Time Limit

  • Each team must complete their attempt within three (3) minutes.

5. Winning Criteria

  • A successful attempt is counted when the ball is balanced and transferred completely from the start line to the end line without falling.
  • The team that completes the task first will be declared the winner (first come, first serve basis).
  • The judges’ and coordinators’ decisions are final and binding on all participants.

6. Key Rules to Remember

  • Do not cross the start line during the attempt.
  • Use only the balls and pens provided by the event coordinators.
  • Hands must not be used to support or balance the ball at any time.
  • Any violation of the rules will result in disqualification.

Rewards & Recognition
